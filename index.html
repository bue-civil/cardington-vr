A typical Category B office fit-out. Open-plan desking for up to 80 people per floor fills the 6.0 m bays along lines 1 to 2 and 3 to 4. Each side of the lift lobby has three glazed meeting rooms and a tea point. The ground floor has a reception lobby under the Level 1 void. The roof is not fitted out.

Press Walk to explore at eye level. Walls, furniture, columns and the facade block your way, and the stairs can be climbed. The entrance slides open, stair and toilet doors swing open as you approach, and the stairs have fire doors with exit signs. Wait by a lift to call it, step in when the doors open, then press E or Q to choose a floor.

With a level selected, only that floor is furnished.

About this model

Grid 45.0 × 21.0 m: five 9.0 m bays (A–F) and three bays of 6.0, 9.0 and 6.0 m (1–4), with core gridlines A′, C′, E′, 2a and 2b.

Framing is taken from the four floor plans. Storey heights are set to 4.34 m at ground and 4.14 m above. X-bracing runs full height in the long direction on line 3 (A–A′, E′–F) and line 2b (C–D), and in the short direction on axes A and F between lines 2b and 3, and on axes C and D between lines 2 and 2b. Deck openings are indicative.

This sample opens read-only. Members cannot be moved, edited or deleted.

Fire drill. Fills every floor with office staff (about two-thirds of desks and some meeting seats occupied). When the alarm sounds, each person waits a few seconds, then walks to the nearer stair core and down to the ground. West-stair users leave by the main entrance and east-stair users by the rear exit on line 4, to assembly points south and north of the building. Lifts return to the ground floor and stop. People do not queue or slow each other down, so the time shown is a best case.
